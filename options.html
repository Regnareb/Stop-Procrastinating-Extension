Search engine exception

When on, clicking through to a specific page on a blocked site straight from a search results page (e.g. a Google search) is allowed through instead of redirected. Landing on just the site's base domain (its homepage, no specific path) is always blocked, even from a search engine — this only exempts deep links to particular content. Trade-off: this still makes it easier to reach content on a blocked site by searching for it.
